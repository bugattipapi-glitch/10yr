MARFA TEXAS · 2026

✦ A small anniversary announcement ✦

We’re going west for ten years.

From Thursday, December 17 through Sunday, December 20, 2026 , we’ll be in Marfa marking a full decade of marriage.

10

What we are doing

A tiny vow renewal.

On December 17—the actual anniversary—we’re planning a small, private renewal of vows. Beyond that, the official itinerary is intentionally unambitious: be in Marfa, take it slow, look at art, eat something good, and celebrate the fact that we still choose each other.

✦

What we are not doing

Turning this into an assignment.

This is not a destination wedding sequel, a command performance, or a test of friendship. We are not hosting a formal group event, and nobody is expected to book a flight, drive across Texas, or rearrange December on our behalf.

✦ · ✧ · ✦

This is an invitation to know—not an obligation to go.

We simply wanted the people we love to know where we’ll be and what we’re celebrating. If a December desert detour happens to sound delightful, lovely. If your contribution is sending a text from the couch, also lovely. There is no attendance test hiding in the fine print.

  • No formal RSVP
  • No registry
  • No guilt

So, in conclusion:

We look forward to seeing you… or not. Either way, you got this invite because we think you rock.

Love, Megan & Jason
